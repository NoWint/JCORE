Private submission is not yet open. The editorial workflow below describes the intended process for future contributors.

1. Prepare Manuscript
2. Read Author Guidelines
3. Submit
4. Editorial Screening
5. Peer Review
6. Revision
7. Editorial Decision
8. Publication

Until private submission opens, corrections, code, and data contributions can be proposed through the journal's public GitHub repository.
