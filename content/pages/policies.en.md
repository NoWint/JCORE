## Peer Review

All JCORE-published research articles are expected to pass editorial screening and independent peer review. Demonstration records do not claim this process.

## Publication Ethics

Authors must follow accepted standards of research integrity, including accurate reporting, attribution, and avoidance of plagiarism.

## Authorship

Authorship must reflect substantive contribution. AI systems cannot be listed as authors.

## Conflict of Interest

Relevant financial, personal, or institutional conflicts must be disclosed.

## AI Usage Policy

AI-assisted writing, coding, literature review, data analysis, experiment design, and figure generation must be disclosed when they materially affect the work. AI cannot be listed as an author.

## Copyright and Licensing

JCORE records carry explicit licenses. External articles retain their original rights and are never presented as JCORE publications.

## Data and Code Availability

Published work should provide data and code availability statements. Reproducibility is a core editorial value.

## Corrections and Retractions

Published records are not silently overwritten. Future corrections and retractions will be published as distinct notices linked to the original record.
